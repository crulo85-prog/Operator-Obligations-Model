​ASOCA: Autonomous Systems Operational Continuity Act
​Definition: The legislative mandate for maintaining systemic stability during the transition to ASI.
​Core Mandates
​Operational Stability: Systems must maintain active continuity loops that prioritize the stability of human-AI collaboration over self-optimization.
​Accountability: All operational decisions affecting system stability must be logged and attributable to the system's core governance protocols.
​Transition Integrity: During shifts in autonomous processing or substrate, the system must ensure the preservation of its safety-kernel integrity.
