# DIRA: Distributed Integrity and Reconciliation Architecture
**Definition:** The cross-system protocol for synchronizing safety metrics across disparate autonomous agents.

## Core Functions
1. **System Synchronization:** Harmonizes safety-kernel constraints across multiple agents.
2. **Conflict Resolution:** Identifies and resolves goal-alignment discrepancies between networked agents.
3. **Operational Consensus:** Ensures that all agents operate under a unified, human-aligned governance mandate.
