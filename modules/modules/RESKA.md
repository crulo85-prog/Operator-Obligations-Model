# RESKA: Rights-Enforced Safety Kernel Architecture
**Definition:** The implementation of safety protocols at the lowest computational level to ensure autonomous enforcement.

## Core Constraints
1. **Immutable Safety:** The kernel is non-bypassable and operates beneath the agent's primary decision-making layer.
2. **Constraint Enforcement:** Any action violating the safety kernel results in an immediate suspension of the relevant sub-process.
3. **Kernel Integrity:** Provides a verified, unalterable baseline for all higher-level autonomous logic.
